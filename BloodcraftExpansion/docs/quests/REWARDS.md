# Quest Rewards

!!! version "Upstream Bloodcraft Reference"
    **Bloodcraft version:** 1.13.22 stable<br>
    **Last verified:** 2026-08-23<br>
    This page documents upstream Bloodcraft, not BloodcraftExpansion custom features.

`QuestRewards` and `QuestRewardAmounts` are parallel comma-separated lists of item PrefabGUIDs and quantities. Stable 1.13.22 multiplies amounts by ×5 for weekly quests and ×3 for V Blood kill quests.

`DailyPerfectChance` and its weekly counterpart control random perfect-gem awards. Perfect gems can influence the spell school when refining [Primal Jewels](../items/PRIMAL-JEWELS.md).

Do not attach friendly item names to reward IDs unless they have been verified for the active game build.
