# Vampiric Dust

!!! version "Upstream Bloodcraft Reference"
    **Bloodcraft version:** 1.13.22 stable<br>
    **Last verified:** 2026-08-23<br>
    This page documents upstream Bloodcraft, not BloodcraftExpansion custom features.

Vampiric Dust is Bloodcraft's familiar-shiny currency. With `ExtraRecipes` enabled, refine 8 Bleeding Hearts and 40 Blood Crystals into 1 Vampiric Dust at an Advanced Grinder.

`ShinyCostItemQuantity` controls the quantity required to make a familiar shiny. Stable 1.13.22 defaults to 100 and clamps the setting to 50–200. Changing an existing shiny effect costs 25% of the configured amount.

Servers that disable `ExtraRecipes` must provide another way to obtain the currency if players are expected to use shiny commands.
